How it works

  1. Paste or upload your referencesAny common format works: APA, IEEE, numbered lists, or a .bib file.
  2. Each entry is looked upCrossRef, DBLP, Semantic Scholar, arXiv and OpenAlex are searched for a matching publication.
  3. Review the differencesOpen any result to compare your citation with the real record, word by word.
  4. Download a corrected .bibFixes are re-validated automatically, and your citation keys stay the same.
